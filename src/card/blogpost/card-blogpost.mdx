### Card Blogpost

A CSS-only component that displays a blogpost teaser card. It embeds a [`card-blogmeta`](?path=/docs/card-blogmeta--docs) for the author/date/read-time line, and the whole card is annotated with schema.org [`BlogPosting`](https://schema.org/BlogPosting) microdata (`headline`, `url`, `description`, plus `author`/`datePublished`/`dateModified`/`timeRequired` from the embedded `card-blogmeta`).
