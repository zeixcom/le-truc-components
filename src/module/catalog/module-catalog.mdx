### Module Catalog

A coordinator component that aggregates the values of several `form-spinbutton` elements into a running total, shown on a shopping-cart badge. It shows how a component can orchestrate others' state without exposing any of its own.

Clicking the cart button also demonstrates validity composition: it re-checks stock for each item in the cart and may lower a spinbutton's `max` (an internally-derived `rangeOverflow`) while separately explaining why via `setCustomValidity()` (an externally-set `customError`) — both coexist on the same `internals` without either clobbering the other. A sold-out item (`max: 0`) is also disabled, which the spinbutton's `fieldset` cascades to its controls natively, and its (still-nonzero) quantity drops out of the total.
