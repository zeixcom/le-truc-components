### Form Radiogroup

A roving-tabindex radio group that works both controlled and uncontrolled. Demonstrates `read()` to initialise `value` from the currently checked radio, and `on('change', ...)` applied to the `radios` Memo so that user interaction propagates back to `host.value`. A `createEffect` on each radio element drives `checked`, `tabIndex`, and the `selected` label class from `host.value` — assigning `host.value = 'option'` programmatically moves the selection without needing a browser event.
