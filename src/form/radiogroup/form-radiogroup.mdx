### Form Radiogroup

A roving-tabindex radio group that works both controlled and uncontrolled. Reads the initially checked radio to seed `value` in `expose()`, and uses `on(radios, 'change', ...)` on the `all()` Memo so user interaction propagates back to `host.value`. Keyboard navigation (Arrow/Home/End keys, click-to-focus) is implemented inline via `on(host, 'keydown'/'keyup'/'click', ...)`, tracking a local `focusIndex` closure variable. `each(radios, ...)` drives `checked`, `tabIndex`, and the `selected` label class per radio from `host.value` — assigning `host.value = 'option'` programmatically moves the selection without needing a browser event. Form participation and validity are via ElementInternals (`formAssociated()`).
