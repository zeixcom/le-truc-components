### Form Tokenbox

A tokenized text input: typed text becomes a removable pill on `,` or when the input loses
focus, gated by the descendant `<input>`'s own constraint validation (`type`, `pattern`,
`maxlength`, …) — an invalid candidate stays in the input and surfaces via `validationMessage`
instead of becoming a pill. Form participation and validity are via ElementInternals
(`formAssociated()`); the exposed `value` is the committed tokens joined by `", "`. External
consumers read `host.validationMessage` / `host.validity` like on a native input, and the inline
`.error` element watches the same reactive `validationMessage` prop.

#### Tag Name

`form-tokenbox`
